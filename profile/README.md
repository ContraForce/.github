ContraForce runs the full security delivery loop — onboarding, triage, investigation,
response, ticketing, reporting, and tuning — across every client, through your operating
procedures. Security Delivery Agents do the work. Gamebooks enforce your procedures.
Your team owns every consequential decision.

Built for MSPs, MSSPs, and internal security teams running Microsoft Sentinel and
Defender XDR.
